FAQ

Questions people
actually ask.

How long does it take?

Most sites are live within two to three weeks. Automations vary — a missed-call text-back can be running in days; a full booking flow takes longer. I'll give you a real timeline on the call, not a guess.

What does it cost?

Depends on the job — a site and an automation cost different things, and every business needs something different. Message me and we'll talk it through.

Do I own the site?

Yes. It's yours — the domain, the hosting, all of it. Nothing is locked behind my account.

What if I need changes later?

Message me. Small changes are usually quick; bigger ones we scope out together before I start.

Do you work outside Kildare?

Yes — all of Ireland, remote. Most of this work never needs an in-person meeting.
